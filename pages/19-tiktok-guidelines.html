Platform Guidelines

TikTok

Sizes for posts & profile visuals

Video1080 × 1920 px
Profile picture200 × 200 px
Cover / thumbnail1080 × 1920 px

Content style

Short, authentic and entertaining video — this is the least polished, most personality‑driven channel. Ride relevant trends and sounds where they genuinely fit.

Tone for posts

Playful, quick, self‑aware. Text overlays are casual, captions are short — the video does the work.

Safe zones & spacing

Keep captions and stickers within the centre‑safe zone: clear of the top 150px (caption bar) and bottom 320px (username, description, action buttons).

Logo usage

Logo appears only as the profile picture (single‑colour mark) — avoid overlaying it on video content itself.

Behind‑the‑scenes content

Shelter walkthroughs, "day in the life" of a volunteer, quick training or feeding clips — this is TikTok's strongest, most differentiating content type.
