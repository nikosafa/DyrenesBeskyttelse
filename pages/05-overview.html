Accounts & channels

Four platforms are covered by this guide, each playing a distinct role in how we reach and engage our audience.

Instagram

Visual storytelling — cute and sad content, reels, adoption pictures.

Facebook

Our broadest community — adoptions, foster & volunteer calls, longer stories.

LinkedIn

Professional audience — partnerships, big donations, job postings.

TikTok

Reach and personality — trends, humour, behind‑the‑scenes video.

Brief overview: what works where

Instagram and Facebook carry most of our emotional, adoption‑driven storytelling; LinkedIn is reserved for professional milestones and fundraising credibility; TikTok is where we experiment, entertain, and reach new, younger audiences. The full breakdown is in the next chapter.
