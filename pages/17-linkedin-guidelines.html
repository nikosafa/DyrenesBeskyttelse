Sizes for posts & profile visuals

Feed image1200 × 627 px
Document / carousel1080 × 1080 px
Logo (square)300 × 300 px
Cover banner1128 × 191 px

Content focus

Professional news, partnerships, results, and organisational milestones — annual impact numbers, major donations, new hires, and job postings.

Tone for posts

Confident and credible, still warm but less playful than Instagram or TikTok. Lead with the outcome or impact, back it up with a number where possible.

Safe zones & spacing

Keep a minimum 40px padding around text in graphics; avoid placing key information in the bottom‑right corner, which is often covered by the reactions bar.

Logo usage

Full‑colour logo on the company page and all official documents; never stretch or recolour the mark.
