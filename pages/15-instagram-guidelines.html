Platform Guidelines

Instagram

Sizes for posts

Feed image (square)1080 × 1080 px
Feed image (portrait)1080 × 1350 px
Reel / Story1080 × 1920 px
Profile picture320 × 320 px

Tone for posts

Emotional and visual‑first. Captions are short and punchy, letting the image or reel carry the story; humour and warmth both work well.

Polls, questions & interactive stickers

Use Story stickers to invite participation — "which foster would you pick?", donation countdown bars, Q&A stickers for AMA‑style sessions with staff.

Visual & emotional storytelling

Favour before/after rescue moments, close‑up portraits, and short reels with an emotional arc (rescue → recovery → adoption).

Safe zones & spacing

For Reels/Stories, keep text and stickers out of the top 250px and bottom 310px to avoid UI overlap. For feed posts, keep a 5% margin on all sides.

Logo usage

Use the single‑colour mark for small placements (profile grid highlights); full colour only when there's no busy background behind it.
