Voice

Guidelines from the current catalogue

Writing principles distilled from Dyrenes Beskyttelse's existing brand catalogue — apply these across every platform, adapting only the level of formality.

How to write

  • Lead with the animal, not the organisation. Start captions with what happened to them, not what we did.
  • Plain language. Write at a level anyone can follow on a first read — no jargon, no internal terminology.
  • Active voice. "We rescued Bella" rather than "Bella was rescued by us."
  • One idea per sentence. Short sentences read better on mobile and in fast‑scrolling feeds.
  • End with a clear next step. Adopt, donate, foster, share — never leave a post without a call to action.
  • Respect the subject. Even sad stories are written with dignity, never for shock value alone.
