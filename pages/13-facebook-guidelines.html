Platform Guidelines

Facebook

Tone for posts

Warm, community‑first and conversational — like an update from a trusted neighbour. Longer captions are welcome here more than on any other platform.

Sizes for posts & profile visuals

Feed image1200 × 630 px
Story / Reel1080 × 1920 px
Profile picture170 × 170 px
Cover photo820 × 312 px

Content types

  • News, campaigns & events
  • Longer‑form stories (rescue journeys, case updates)
  • Adoption posts, foster & volunteer calls
  • Reels for lighter, shareable moments

Linking out

Every campaign or adoption post should link to the relevant page: the main website, the donation page, or the specific adoption listing — never just the homepage.

Answering questions & comments

  • Reply within 24 hours, in the same warm tone as our posts
  • Adoption status questions: confirm availability before replying publicly
  • Never argue in public — move disputes to Messenger

Safe zones & spacing

Keep key text and faces inside the centre 80% of the frame for posts; for Stories/Reels keep a 250px margin top and bottom clear of UI elements.

Logo usage

Use the white reversed logo over photos; full colour on plain backgrounds only. Minimum clear space equal to the logo's own height on all sides.
