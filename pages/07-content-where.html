What content works where?

Written by Lova

Use this as a quick filter before you create anything: does this content type belong on this platform?

Instagram

  • Cute & sad content
  • Reels
  • Adoption pictures

Facebook

  • Cute & sad content
  • Post‑adoption stories
  • Reels
  • Foster & volunteer calls

LinkedIn

  • Business updates
  • Large donations
  • Job postings
  • Organisational info

TikTok

  • Trends
  • Humour
  • Short‑form video

What not to do

  • Disney‑style illustration or animation
  • Dressing animals in clothing
  • Featuring animals or breeds with known health challenges
  • Chasing unrelated trends (e.g. "puppy yoga" events)
  • Personifying animals (human speech, human behaviour)
